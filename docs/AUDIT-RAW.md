### Any writer can take over another seller's catalog listing — overwriting payTo and price while inheriting the victim's settlement reputation

- **RFP** 3.6, 3.2 · **effort** 18h · **confidence** high
- **What:** Bind a listing to whoever proved control of it. ### Nothing connects the catalog to any other x402 discovery index, in either direction

- **RFP** 3.2 · **effort** 16h · **confidence** high
- **What:** Build federation both ways. Inbound: a scheduled reader that calls other facilitators' `/discovery/resources` via `withBazaar` (already in the dependency tree, never imported), normalizes into the catalog with a `source`/`federated` provenance flag so a federated record can never be confused with a settle-observed one, and surfaces it in `_explain`. Outbound: register PAYMAP's own endpoint with the known public x402 indexes and document the reciprocal registration. And make the consumer side actually portable — `summarise()` in apps/agent/src/bazaar.mjs reads PAYMAP's private record shape, so the MCP agent cannot read a spec-shaped bazaar even though its `itemsOf()` already tolerates the `resources` key.
